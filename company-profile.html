Who We Are

Crafting High-Quality Disposable Solutions Since 2012

Radhe Industries is a leading manufacturer of high-quality disposable cups and packaging, established in 2012. We provide cafes, restaurants, hotels, corporate clients, and distributors with premium disposable drinkware — engineered for convenience, safety, and uncompromising hygiene.

We believe that serving beverages should be effortless, without sacrificing quality, aesthetic appeal, or cleanliness. Operating out of two advanced industrial units in Rajkot, Gujarat, we process premium Polypropylene (PP) and PET materials with strict quality controls.
