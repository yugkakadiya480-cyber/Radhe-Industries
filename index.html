How We Work

Our Manufacturing Process

Every cup passes through a rigorous 4-step process to guarantee perfection.

Raw Material Sourcing
Premium food-grade polymers and eco-friendly raw materials sourced from certified suppliers across India.
Precision Manufacturing
State-of-the-art thermoforming machines at our 2 units in Metoda and Devda, Rajkot ensure consistent quality.
Strict Quality Check
Every batch is rigorously tested for strength, food safety, and dimensional accuracy before packaging.
Swift Delivery
Packed securely and dispatched via our pan-India logistics network for on-time delivery at your doorstep.
