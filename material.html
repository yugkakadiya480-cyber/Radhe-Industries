Polypropylene (PP)

Polypropylene is our primary material for hot and cold beverage cups. Known for its excellent heat resistance, crystal clarity, and full recyclability.

  • Heat Resistant up to 120°C
  • 100% Recyclable & Eco-Friendly
  • BPA-Free & Food Safe (FSSAI)
  • Sourced from Certified Suppliers

PET (Polyethylene Terephthalate)

PET granules are used for our premium transparent cups — offering crystal-clear visibility, perfect for cold coffees, juices, mocktails, and desserts.

  • Superior Crystal Clarity
  • High Impact & Shatter Resistance
  • Widely Recyclable
  • Zero Taste/Odour Transfer
